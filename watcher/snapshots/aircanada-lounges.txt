Maple Leaf Lounge Characteristics
The following customers are invited to enjoy the comfort and many amenities of our 23 award-winning lounges:
Business and First Class Ticket Holders
Enjoy access to our Maple Leaf Lounge! Just present your valid same-day ticket or boarding pass*.
Guests are also welcome to join you for a fee of 59.00 CAD, USD, EUR or GBP**.
Departing on an Emirates flight in Business or First Class? You have access to International Maple Leaf Lounges at Toronto Pearson (YYZ) and Montréal-Trudeau (YUL) airports.
**Fee currency is based on lounge location and is subject to local taxes.
Aeroplan Super Elite, Aeroplan 75K and Aeroplan 50K Members
Start enjoying access to our Maple Leaf Lounge! Just provide your Aeroplan number and valid same-day ticket or boarding pass*.
Your partner, one guest and up to 5 dependent children** can join you, free of charge. Additional guests are welcome for a fee of 59.00 CAD, USD, EUR or GBP***.
Arriving on an Air Canada, Air Canada Rouge or Air Canada Express flight? You can also access domestic Maple Leaf Lounges in Canada and the U.S.
If you’re departing on an Emirates flight in Business or First Class, you have access to international Maple Leaf Lounges at Toronto Pearson (YYZ) and Montréal-Trudeau (YUL) airports.
**Children under the age of 25 must be part of the family unit and financially dependent on the Aeroplan Super Elite 100K/Aeroplan 75K/Aeroplan 50K Member. Air Canada reserve the right to request proof of age for dependent children at any time.
*** Fee currency is based on lounge location and is subject to local taxes.
Star Alliance Gold Members
Enjoy access to our Maple Leaf Lounge! Just present your Star Alliance Gold membership card and valid same-day ticket or boarding pass*.
You can invite one guest traveling on the same flight at no extra charge. Additional guests are welcome for a fee of 59.00 CAD, USD, EUR or GBP**.
** Fee currency is based on lounge location and is subject to local taxes.

Can be used by Primary Cardholders, Secondary Cardholders, or any guest when accompanied by qualified cardholder.

Complimentary light snacks and beverages
Enjoy a selection refreshing beverages, such as juice, soda, tea, coffee and specialty coffees - including espresso, cappuccino and latte. Also enjoy a selection of beer, wine, spirits and delicious snacks.
Our lounges are fully stocked with popular reading materials catering to a wide range of interests. Don’t feel like reading? Sit back, relax, and watch your favourite TV shows.

Business Centres
