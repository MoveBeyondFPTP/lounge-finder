Access to The Centurion® Lounge Network
Please review the below information for Centurion® Lounge policies around admittance for Card Members and their guests. These policies are applicable to Centurion Lounge locations in the U.S., London, Hong Kong, Tokyo, and Amsterdam, including Sidecar by The Centurion® Lounge. Access policies may vary between international locations; please visit the Centurion Lounge homepage and select the international Centurion Lounge location you plan to visit for location-specific policy details.
Card Member Access Policy
To access The Centurion® Lounge or Sidecar by The Centurion® Lounge, Card Members must bring:
Valid Consumer Platinum Card, Business Platinum Card, or Corporate Platinum Card
Confirmed same-day boarding pass with seat/zone assignment
Name on Card and boarding pass must match. Card Members may be asked to present the Centurion Lounge agent with a government-issued I.D. as a part of the check-in process. We are happy to admit Platinum Card Members into locations of The Centurion® Lounge in the U.S., London, Hong Kong, Tokyo, and Amsterdam within 3 hours of the departure time stated on your same-day, confirmed boarding pass. We can’t admit you if you’ve just landed, unless you have a layover or connecting flight that departs on the same day. During a layover, Card Members must arrive within 5 hours of their connecting flight. Same-day round-trip itineraries are not considered connecting flights. At Sidecar by The Centurion® Lounge (LAS), all Platinum Card Members must enter within 90 minutes of the departure time stated on your same-day, confirmed boarding pass.
Guest Access Policy
U.S. Consumer Platinum Card and U.S. Business Platinum Card Members
$50 fee for each guest 18 and over at U.S. Centurion Lounges (£35,00 per guest at the London Centurion Lounge, €45 per guest at the Amsterdam Centurion Lounge, HKD 380 per guest at the Hong Kong Centurion Lounge, and JPY 8,300 per guest at the Tokyo Centurion Lounge)
$30 for children aged 2-17 at U.S. Centurion Lounges (£24,00 per child at the London Centurion Lounge, €25 per child at the Amsterdam Centurion Lounge, HKD 230 per child at the Hong Kong Centurion Lounge, and JPY 5,000 per child at the Tokyo Centurion Lounge). Under 2 is complimentary provided an accompanying parent or guardian can show a lap infant boarding pass or proof of age
All guests must be traveling on the same flight as the Card Member.
U.S. Consumer Platinum Card and U.S. Business Platinum Card Members may qualify for Complimentary Guest Access for up to 2 complimentary guests per visit after spending $75,000 in eligible purchases on your Card within the calendar year. After Complimentary Guest Access has been unlocked, it will be available to you for the remainder of the calendar year in which it was attained, the following calendar year, and until January 31 of the next calendar year (for example, if you attain Complimentary Guest Access on May 1, 2025, it will remain effective for you through January 31, 2027). Once you have unlocked Complimentary Guest Access, any additional guests over the limit of two complimentary guests will be subject to the guest rates above.
A physical American Express Card must be used as the payment method for all paid guest access.

Up to two complimentary guests per visit

International Platinum Card Members
One complimentary guest per visit.

Guest access policies may vary internationally by location and are subject to change. For more information on international Centurion Lounge locations, please visit the Centurion Lounge homepage and select the international Centurion Lounge location you plan to visit. Jump seats are not confirmed seat assignments. Airline, airport, and industry employees are not permitted access while in uniform, wearing branded apparel, or company and airport identification badges, including employees of private and corporate aircraft and charter carriers. All access is subject to space availability. Same-day round trip flights are not considered connecting flights. Additional terms apply.
