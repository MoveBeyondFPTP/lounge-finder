Chase lists seven open Sapphire Lounges by The Club in the U.S., in Boston, Dallas, Las Vegas, New York LaGuardia, Philadelphia, Phoenix and San Diego. The New York JFK lounge is temporarily closed, and Los Angeles is listed as coming soon, with no date. Sapphire Reserve cardmembers walk in with up to two guests for free, and Sapphire Preferred holders don't get complimentary access. Everything below comes from Chase's lounge page and card pages as they read on October 20, 2026, so check the page again before a tight connection.
The Seven Open Lounges
Here's each open lounge with the terminal and hours Chase gives for it:
Boston Logan (BOS): Terminal B, near gate B40, 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily.
Dallas Fort Worth (DFW): Terminal D, near gate D25, 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.
Las Vegas Harry Reid (LAS): Terminal 1, near gate C23, 4:30 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily.
New York LaGuardia (LGA): Terminal B, on the bridge before gates 40 to 59, 4:30 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. daily.
Philadelphia (PHL): Terminal D/E Connector, 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.
Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX): Terminal 4, near gate D11, 4:30 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. daily.
San Diego (SAN): Terminal 2, near gates 46 and 47, 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.
Also on Chase's Page
A few more entries sit on the same page without being an eighth open Sapphire Lounge. The New York JFK lounge, in Terminal 4 near gate A2 and named by Chase as the lounge "with Etihad Airways," is listed as temporarily closed, and Chase prints no hours for it. The Reserve Suites by Chase are private rooms inside the LaGuardia lounge, reserved for a fee at least 72 hours before your flight. At Washington Dulles, Chase points Reserve cardmembers to The Etihad Lounge on Concourse A, near gate A14, open 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily. You show an eligible Chase card or Chase-issued Priority Pass Select membership and a boarding pass for a departing flight.
Los Angeles (LAX) is listed as coming soon. For Seattle, our report on the proposed Seattle-Tacoma lounge covers the plan and the lease vote.
Chase says primary cardmembers and authorized users of the Sapphire Reserve, the J.P. Morgan Reserve and The Ritz-Carlton Credit Card have access to the Sapphire Lounges. Sapphire Reserve for Business primary cardmembers get in too, and its employee cardmembers don't.
Each cardmember can bring up to two guests for free, and each additional guest costs $27. You enter up to three hours before a departing flight with a boarding pass and either your physical card or your digital Chase-issued Priority Pass Select membership. Chase's Priority Pass terms add that a credit card in your digital wallet won't be accepted at a lounge.
On the Preferred side, Chase's lounge page says complimentary lounge access is not a Sapphire Preferred benefit. Our answer to whether the Chase Sapphire Preferred has lounge access covers what you get instead.
How the Lounges Fit the Reserve
The lounges come with the Chase Sapphire Reserve, which carries a $795 annual fee, along with the Priority Pass Select membership Chase states covers 1,300+ lounges. Chase also lists the lounge benefit's value in its terms, but the better test is your own airports. For how the lounges sit beside Chase Travel and transfers, see our guide to the Chase travel stack.
If your home airport or your usual connection is one of the seven, the Reserve's lounge access is something you'd use every trip. If it isn't, the Priority Pass membership is the part you'd rely on, and the lounge list above is a bonus on the trips that touch it.
